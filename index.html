Load-adaptive KV-cache sharing

NVIDIA x SIT AI Center · research intern · Summer 2026

the problem

Multi-agent LLM apps resend a lot of the same context. Recomputing the KV cache every time makes the first token slow, and the speedup tricks need hand-tuned settings.

what I built

A serving layer over NVIDIA's Retail Shopping Assistant Blueprint. I reproduced CacheBlend, swept static recompute ratios, then built a controller that picks the ratio on its own and lands on the hand-tuned value without anyone tuning it.

what changed

About 4× faster time to first token with no drop in answer quality (F1 parity on MuSiQue).
